How to subscribe in Google Calendar
  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar → Add calendar → From URL.
  2. Paste the feed address above and select Add calendar.

The Google Calendar phone app can't add feeds directly — subscribe once on the web and it syncs to your phone automatically. You may need to open the calendar app on your phone and set the calendar to sync to have it show up initially.

How to subscribe in Apple Calendar

On an Apple device, tap a "Subscribe via webcal" link above to open your calendar with the subscription ready to confirm.

Or add it by hand:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste the feed address.
  • Mac: in the Calendar app, File → New Calendar Subscription, then paste the feed address.
How to subscribe in Outlook
  1. In Outlook on the web, choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  2. Paste the feed address and choose Import.
